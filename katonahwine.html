Katonah Wine & Liquor

Social Media Marketing / 2026

  • Digital Marketing
  • Content Strategy
  • Social Media Design

Katonah Wine & Liquor is an independent wine and spirits shop in Westchester, New York, built around local community and discovery. I developed a social media and marketing system that gave the store a cohesive visual identity, with content connecting its products, events, and people to the experience of shopping local.

The Brief

The store wanted a stronger Instagram presence and more people at its tastings and events, but the account was mostly straightforward bottle photography with little visual consistency. With wide creative freedom, I took on the visual direction and content strategy across posts, Stories, Reels, and product photography, and extended the system into monthly email templates the store could maintain on its own.

Strategy

Research into how independent shops build community online pointed to education, personality, and experience over promotion alone, so I organized the content around four pillars: education and discovery, community and personality, products and new arrivals, and events. A flexible system of type, grid, color, and layout kept everything consistent, and I shot much of the photo and video myself, staging products in Hudson Valley settings.

Results

The account grew its followers by 20% in two months, with 53% of views coming from non-followers. Instagram and email now share one recognizable visual language, and the simpler, repeatable email templates let the store keep producing campaigns independently. Co-branded campaigns with four local businesses tie the store’s online presence back to its in-person community.
