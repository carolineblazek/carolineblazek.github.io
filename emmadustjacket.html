Emma Reimagined

Dust Jacket ✸ Design

Year

2025

Client

Typography II

Skills

Staged Photography, Composition, Color

This dust jacket for Emma by Jane Austen reimagines the novel through staged photography and thoughtful object selection.

Printed type, sheer fabric, glass, vines, pearls, and ribbons were arranged across multiple iterations to reflect the playful tone of the story while highlighting key characteristics of Emma’s personality. Supplemental information, including the blurb, reviews, and author's description, was set with intentional choices in typeface and a light, feminine color palette. The final design balances narrative, materiality, and visual cohesion to create a jacket that is both expressive and intriguing, inviting interaction.
