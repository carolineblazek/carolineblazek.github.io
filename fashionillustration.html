Client

Fashion Drawing

Skills

Technical Flats, Croquis, Material Experimentation

These fashion illustrations tell the story of two collections with distinct moods and personalities.

Lotuses in Radiant Bloom captures serenity and grace through soft pastels and airy linework, while Sassy and Chic conveys confidence and boldness with striking black-and-white contrast. Across both collections, I experiment with a wide variety of material, from watercolor and pen to beads, fabric, and digital editing, using texture and color to bring each design to life.
