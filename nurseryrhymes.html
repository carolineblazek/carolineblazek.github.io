Nursery Rhyme Posters

Posters ✸ Illustration

Year

2025

Client

Word & Image II

Skills

Visual Storytelling, Composition, Color

These Nursery Rhyme posters bring story to life through elegant Art Nouveau–inspired compositions and a gentle, calming color palette.

Each poster uses floral motifs and simple arrangements to connect nature with narrative, reflecting the story of the rhyme. Research into ex-libris bookplates of the Art Nouveau age informed the ornamental structures and layouts of each poster, while textural effects were applied to give the prints a tactile, handcrafted quality. The series balances decorative detail and readability, resulting in cohesive visuals that celebrate both the whimsy of the rhymes and their connection to the natural world.
