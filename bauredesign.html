Beacon Artist Union

Brand Identity / 2025

  • Logo Design
  • Visual Systems
  • Advertising

The Beacon Artist Union (BAU) is an artist-run gallery in the Hudson Valley focused on experimentation, collaboration, and community. I developed a new visual identity that reflects the gallery’s energetic, open approach to contemporary art and flexes across exhibitions, promotional materials, and the surrounding community.

The Brief

BAU’s programming is diverse and experimental, so its identity needed to be equally dynamic. Rather than a rigid system built on a single style, I set out to make an identity that could adapt to different artists and disciplines while staying recognizably BAU, with enough presence to hold up across physical and digital applications.

The System

I simplified the gallery’s abbreviated name into a graphic mark that works as both a logo and a recurring visual element, then built a vibrant palette and used shifts in scale, color, and composition to create hierarchy and movement. Selected gallery works enter the system through color treatments and overlays, so each artist stays distinct within a consistent frame.

In Use

The identity moves between bold, energetic applications and quieter presentations of artwork. I applied it across posters, banners, digital advertising, tickets, stickers, and business cards, showing how it holds together across scales and contexts while keeping the gallery, its artists, and its community connected.
