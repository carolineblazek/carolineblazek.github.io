The Living Pattern

Book ✸ Design

Year

2025

Client

Typography II

Skills

Pacing, Layout Design, Typographic Systems

This book design contrasts The Lesser Arts by William Morris and Questions of Style by Alois Riegl through two distinct typographic systems informed by their historical contexts.

Morris’s text is interpreted through an Art Nouveau–inspired approach, with flowing, overlapping letterforms that evoke the tactile qualities of ironwork and other decorative materials. Riegl’s writing is expressed through a visual language influenced by Viennese Secessionism, organizing text into structured compartments and abstracting letterforms into geometric shapes. Together, the systems create a visual dialogue that mirrors the authors' differing perspectives on style, ornament, and modernity.
