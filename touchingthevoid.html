Touching The Void Exhibition Poster

Poster ✸ Design

Year

2025

Client

Typography II

Skills

Grid-Based Layout, Type As Image, Digital Editing

This exhibition poster for Touching the Void at the Museum of Modern Art responds to the show’s emphasis on abstraction, bare form, and structural clarity.

The title is treated as image through a textural, distorted interpretation in grayscale, while supplementary information, artist names and gallery talks, are organized within a rigid grid, creating a dialogue between expressive typography and structured layout. To achieve this high-contrast black-and-white imagery I physically manipulated paper through tearing, cutting, and wrinkling, then digitally refined the compositions in Photoshop.
