Valentine's Gift Set

Gift Set ✸ Illustration

Year

2024

Client

Illustration Entrepreneur

Skills

Pattern Design, Hand Lettering, Line Quality

This Valentine’s gift set, including a gift bag, wrapping paper, and a bag tag, was developed with considerations of cohesion and romantic nostalgia.

The gift bag uses a pen-based contour style inspired by fashion illustration of the mid-twentieth-century, while the wrapping paper renders a repeat pattern of roses and ribbons with a soft, inky texture. The bag tag features delicate hand-lettering, framed by a ribbon border. Across the set, I refined composition and texture to create a unified romantic mood that flows consistently across all three items.
