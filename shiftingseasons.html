Shifting Seasons

Card Catalog ✸ Illustration

Year

Fall 2025 (3 weeks)

Client

Word & Image II

Tools

Procreate, InDesign

Skills

Data Visualization, Info Hierarchy, Interaction

This card series was developed to translate complex EPA data on climate change-induced shifts in first bloom into an accessible, educational format.

I explored how color and composition could communicate different types of information across the 16 cards, balancing context, data, and narrative. The six map cards are designed to form a full geographical display of the data, introducing interactivity that supports exploration and hands-on learning. Throughout the project, I focused on engagement and understanding, iterating multiple approaches to presenting the data in ways that are clear, informative, and visually cohesive.
