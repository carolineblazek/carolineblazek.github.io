Vessels & Vines

Drawings ✸ Illustration

Year

2024

Client

Illustration Entrepreneur

Skills

Gesture, Color, Figurative Composition

This series examines the figure within a constrained, square format, using the vase as a central structural element.

Each illustration explores how posture and gesture shift when the body interacts with a fixed object, with foliage and flowers used to define spatial boundaries and balance the composition. A limited blue and pink palette establishes cohesion across the set, while variation in pose and floral arrangement introduces contrast. The series emphasizes control of space, form, and the relationship between figure, ornament, and frame.
