Calendar Pages ✸ Illustration

Year

2024

Client

Illustration Entrepreneur

Skills

Figurative Illustration, Gesture, Narrative

This calendar series explores the figure in motion to celebrate the everyday activities characteristic of each season.

Gesture, posture, and composition were used to convey energy and playfulness, while soft, seasonal pastel palettes were developed through iterative color studies to reflect the unique mood of each month. This project emphasizes experimentation with rhythm and figural storytelling, creating a cohesive series that highlights the joy and peace of each season while maintaining visual harmony across the several pages.
